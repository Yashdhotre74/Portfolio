My approach

Engineering becomes real when the plan reaches the site.

On site, I have learned to look beyond a drawing: understand the sequence of work, check dimensions and quantities, coordinate with the team, and keep records that help the next activity move forward.

I am still learning every day. I try to ask the right questions, verify details before work proceeds, and connect what I learned during my diploma and B.Tech with actual construction conditions.

What matters to me: safe execution, clear communication, accurate records, and being dependable when the site team needs information.
