My approach

Clear Inputs. Traceable Calculations. Useful Results.

For me, estimation is more than arriving at a number. It is about understanding the scope, showing how a quantity was calculated, and making the result useful for planning and execution.

Where relevant, I connect quantity work with actual site progress, material requirements, and construction records—because estimates are most useful when they reflect the work being carried out.
