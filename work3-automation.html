My approach

I Start With a Real Task, Then Build the Sheet Around It.

My approach is practical: understand what the site team needs to record, identify the inputs and units, build the formula or entry structure, and test the result against a manual check. A spreadsheet should make the work clearer — not make the user guess how it works.

For example, I have been organising material-receipt entries with supplier selection and automatic entry IDs, and building formula-based records for equipment hours and civil quantity calculations. I continue refining these tools as the requirements of site work become clearer.

  • ✓
    Understand the site requirementDecide what must be recorded or calculated, and which details cannot be missed.
  • ✓
    Keep the calculation visibleUse clear formulas, consistent units, and straightforward layouts that another person can follow.
  • ✓
    Check before relying on the resultCompare outputs with a manual calculation or known site value, and review unusual results.
  • ✓
    Make the record useful laterKeep dates, references, suppliers, and notes organised for review and follow-up.
